# MVP Research Question

Can ecological claims about an invasive species and its interactions with native species be represented as machine-testable objects, continuously re-evaluated as new evidence arrives, and routed through a calibrated decision layer that determines whether to continue monitoring, investigate, escalate, or gather additional evidence?

## Initial Case Study

- Invasive species: spotted lanternfly (`Lycorma delicatula`)
- Region: U.S. Mid-Atlantic
- Interaction target: tree of heaven and native hardwood/agricultural edge habitats
- Time range: fixture data covers 2019-2026
- Confounders: sampling effort, public-reporting bias, temperature anomalies, host availability
